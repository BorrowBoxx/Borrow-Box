Campus Safety & Lending Terms

Welcome to BorrowBox! Before sharing or requesting items, please confirm your agreement to our campus guidelines:

1. Directory Only: BorrowBox is a free, student-built directory. We do not inspect, insure, or guarantee any item listed.

2. Personal Responsibility: Lending and borrowing is done entirely at your own risk. Any item damage, loss, or return dispute is solely between you and the other student.

3. Prohibited Stuff: Listing illegal substances, dangerous tools, stolen property, or exam-cheating materials is strictly forbidden.

4. Peer Payments: Any voluntary rental price or deposit is paid directly peer-to-peer (via UPI or cash). BorrowBox handles zero money and takes zero fees.

Terms & Safety Policy

1. Role of BorrowBox
BorrowBox operates as an intermediary peer-to-peer directory under applicable IT Guidelines. We do not manufacture, inspect, own, or store any physical items.

2. Bailment & Responsibility
All arrangements regarding item handover, duration, return condition, and care are mutual agreements solely between the lender and borrower.

3. Content Removal
Any listing reported for violating campus policy or student safety will be removed immediately without prior notice.

4. Privacy
Your public campus handle is shown to peers when listing or inquiring. We do not sell or share user data with third-party marketers.
